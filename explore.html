Inside the constraint

My rules give one answer, but most letters can be found in several note names. The table sets the limits; within them, I choose by ear.

Each letter below lists the notes it can become. A to G always stay themselves, except that B can also be B♭, as in German, and K, R, T, U and Y each have one fixed note. Other letters count only if they begin a note name or are its second letter, as in eS or mI, and my own rules are always included. Change any of them and listen to the difference.
